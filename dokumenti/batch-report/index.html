Natural Sediment

Natural sediment may occur in concentrated hydroalcoholic mushroom extracts. This sediment is not residual mushroom material. Individual extraction fractions are filtered prior to blending. Following combination of the concentrated aqueous and alcoholic fractions, components of the polysaccharide-rich fraction may partially precipitate in the presence of ethanol. This is considered a normal physical characteristic of this type of concentrated liquid extract.
