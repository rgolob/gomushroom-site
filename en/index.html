Why are GoMushroom tinctures different?

Made as an extract.
Not mixed from powder.

Many suppliers bottle tinctures from bought-in extracts or from powder stirred into alcohol. At GoMushroom the extract itself is made here — a genuine native extract, from a mushroom we grew to the bottle you receive.

Our own raw material

We cultivate the medicinal mushrooms ourselves, which is how we know their origin, how they grew and what quality went into the extraction.

Multi-stage extraction

Ultrasound, hot water and pressurised extraction one after another, so the extract carries a wider range of compounds than any single step can reach.

Vacuum concentration

The extract is concentrated under reduced pressure at low temperature, so the solvent leaves without cooking the compounds we went to the trouble of extracting.

Transparency

We show publicly how the extract is made, and every batch carries its own designation and stays traceable.

Natural sediment is not a fault. In highly concentrated tinctures, part of the dissolved matter may no longer stay fully in solution in the final solvent. That is a sign of a saturated solution rather than of poor filtration.

Approach — triple extraction and vacuum concentration

Step 1

Triple extraction

Ethanol → hot water → pressure water. Each stage targets a different spectrum, resulting in a full-spectrum extract.

Step 2

High solvent volume

A higher solvent volume delays saturation, improves mass transfer and increases extraction yield.

Step 3

Ultrasound support

Ultrasound micro-cavitation loosens the matrix and improves release of compounds — faster extraction, higher yield.

Step 4

Vacuum concentration

We remove solvents at lower temperatures to preserve sensitive compounds, natural aroma and color.

QC 1

Quality

Internal QC on raw materials and final products: solids, density, extractable fraction and batch traceability.

QC 2

Compliance

Work follows HACCP principles and a GMP approach; ethanol use is recorded with excise-compliant tracking.

About Rok Golob — GoMushroom

Rok Golob — GoMushroom

My name is Rok Golob and I am the founder of GoMushroom.

For nearly 20 years, I have worked in an analytical laboratory environment, where I gained extensive practical experience in laboratory analytics, GMP systems, process thinking, and chromatographic techniques and analytical methods. Part of this knowledge I also share within the laboratory environment today.

Analytical work teaches discipline very quickly — a result is not valuable because it looks good on paper, but because the method is reproducible, the sampling makes sense, and the process is under control.

My approach to work comes from analytics and process thinking. My experience in laboratory analytics now naturally connects with the development of extraction processes, formulations, and a deeper understanding of the complex mechanisms behind medicinal mushrooms.

That same way of thinking is what I bring into GoMushroom.

What has always fascinated me most is the technical and engineering side of extraction: selecting suitable solvents, protecting sensitive compounds, and designing processes that remain efficient, stable, and reproducible over time. Much of my work is built around understanding extraction processes, concentration techniques, compound solubility, and the interpretation of analytical results.

GoMushroom was never created as a fast trend, but as a project built on practice, systems, and attention to detail.

Working in small batches allows me to maintain control over the entire process — from raw material selection to the final formulation. I believe that a high-quality product is not the result of marketing or luck, but of a well-designed process, clear control points, and consistency in execution.

When raw materials, extraction, formulation, measurements, and documentation all come together correctly, the result is a product built on a system — not on chance.

That is also the essence of GoMushroom.
